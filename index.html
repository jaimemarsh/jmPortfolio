About Me

Web Developer & Manager | Photographer | Social Media & Marketing Manager

Hello! My name is Jaime Marsh, I currently work at Market Bar Bridge Park as the Social Media Coordinator, based in Dublin, OH. I have experience in many different fields including Sports Medicine, Bartending, Gymnastics and Cheerleading, Website Developing, being a Lead at Walt Disney World, and more. My background gives me a wide range of not only work experience, but life experience, that pushes me forward to new opportunities. I started at Market Bar a year ago after working in Healthcare. I had a passion and drive for creativity I needed to explore, and I am very proud to see all the things I have done a year later. I cannot wait to see what I do a year from now!

In my free time I explore creativity in photography and art, as well as continue to educate myself to better my work. I enjoy the outdoors and being active. I have two cats at home, and a fiance, that continues to drive me to do what I’m passionate about. I love color, and I strive to give people a fun and new perspective on products and mundane life. Thank you for your time looking at my work! Feel free to contact me anytime.
